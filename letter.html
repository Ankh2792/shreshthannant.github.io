A Letter to Shreshtha

To my love, my soulmate, my best friend, my Shreshtha,

I have written you many letters, and we can count at least 31 of them together. In each and every one, I express my love for you, my desire that we remain together forever, that we grow with each other and that we spend all of eternity in each others arms. But I have had my shortcomings and flaws. This letter is not me apologising for them, but rather as a callback to simpler times. I have written all my letters to you as your lover so far, and today is no different. The difference today is that I am now a lover who is reflecting on our shared past, and wants to tell you his story. Because in my letters, in my poems, in my actions, there has never been a why. Today there will be.

I want to tell you your story in my life, how you stormed in and cemented your place in my heart. And it begins from the days of rehearsals for Diwali Ball. During those rehearsals, to me it felt we clicked so quickly and we gelled so well as people. I remember us creating the moves in paoori together, the putting rang on each other and spin, well your idea, and just how it felt so easy to do with you. I remember how we'd bicker like an old married couple even back then, and I remember Akshata even making a comment about it. I remember when you scolded me after I said I wouldn't be using Wolfson even though I had explicitly asked you to book it for me. I remember how it made me feel, how inconveniencing you affected me. I remember how you suggested that I pay you back by cooking for you, and how excited I was to do that. And concerning that, at the end of one of the rehearsals in the buttery, you mentioned it to the others, and I remember thinking that I didnt want to extend the invite to anybody but you. And during those rehearsals, you told me you were falling ill and I made you chai for your cold. And in that same rehearsal, I asked you to sit with me and help me give feedback for everyone, because I felt that we complemented each other perfectly in that regard, and I was right to think that in general too. These events at rehearsals along with how effortless dancing felt with you sparked the love I have for you.

Then came the time we spent together after the ball. Honestly, there is so much to your story that condesing it into one letter will not do it justice, because the impact and extent of your story in my life is immeasurable. Therefore, I shall make this letter the first of many parts of the telling of our story. And there are many more to come my love.

But one thing I want to tell you about is my why. I remember when we made our playlist together, I put Jaane Kaise in the playlist. We created the playlist on 23rd January, the day you had left Oxford, and we made it while you were on the way to the airport. And Jaane Kaise was one of the first songs I added to the playlist. When we got together, you asked me why I added it. I told you then, and I shall tell you now. Before you, I imagined it as my first dance, but there was nobody opposite me. Then I met you, and gradually, every time I listened to it, the person I began imagining sharing my first dance was you. And it was you when I added the song into the playlist. It was the first time I sang it to you. And it still is today. I want to spend the rest of my life with you my love, I am sure of it. You complete parts of me I never knew even existed. You light up the darkness in my life, and make the other parts shine even brighter. You make me me, more than I ever could. And I love you so much

You are incredibly special to me. And I love you so much.

Signed, your lovey, your love, your best friend, your soulmate, your Annant
